Interpretation notice

The results below are exploratory statistical associations from a limited World Bank dataset. They are not causal findings, verified discoveries, evidence of novelty or peer-reviewed conclusions. Strong correlations can reflect broad development patterns, confounding variables and shared underlying factors.

Baseline snapshot · 23 November 2026

Initial cross-country indicator scan, 2000–2023

The prototype requested eight indicators and retrieved seven successfully. It calculated yearly Pearson correlations across countries, requiring at least 40 observations per year and at least eight valid years per indicator pair.

Data quality note

One indicator was not retrieved

The carbon-dioxide emissions-per-capita request returned an unexpected API response and was excluded from this cycle. The current pipeline therefore did not test relationships involving that indicator.

What happens next?

Candidate signals are starting points, not endpoints.

The next meaningful step is not to publish more correlations from the same narrow dataset. It is to investigate alternative explanations, improve source coverage, compare independent datasets and design tests that could reject an apparent relationship.
