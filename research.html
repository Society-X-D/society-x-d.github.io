How to read this page

The correlations are exploratory associations, not causal effects or verified discoveries. Research priority scores combine repeatability, estimator agreement, data coverage and warning penalties; they are transparent heuristics, not probabilities or measures of importance. OpenAlex hit counts are bounded literature-search context, not systematic reviews or proof of novelty. The same relationship can appear in more than one field because scientific questions often cross disciplinary boundaries. Sample counts are country-year observations summed across years, not unique countries.
