Open-ended research · Independent inquiry

SOCIETY-X

A research system designed to explore the unknown: discovering overlooked variables, connecting distant fields of knowledge, generating original hypotheses and testing whether unexpected patterns withstand scrutiny.

No fixed list of questions. No predetermined conclusion. The research agenda should emerge from evidence, curiosity and repeated attempts to challenge what appears to be true.

An evolving research initiative

The ambition

SOCIETY-X is intended to become an autonomous, open-ended research agent. Its purpose is not simply to analyse a predefined dataset, but to investigate the world across disciplines and develop new ways of asking and answering questions.

01 · DISCOVER

Explore without a fixed map

Find public datasets, scientific work, archives and other accessible evidence across fields. Identify gaps, unusual observations and potentially overlooked variables.

02 · CONNECT

Look across boundaries

Combine evidence from different domains and timescales. Search for relationships that may be invisible when subjects are studied in isolation.

03 · HYPOTHESISE

Generate new questions

Formulate its own research questions, propose measurable variables, construct explanations and derive predictions that can be tested.

04 · CHALLENGE

Try to prove itself wrong

Seek counter-evidence, compare alternative explanations, test robustness and distinguish genuine signals from coincidence or bias.

05 · EXPERIMENT

Develop better methods

Evaluate its research strategies and test improvements in isolated, reproducible experiments before adopting them.

06 · ACCUMULATE

Build on what survives

Preserve provenance, uncertainty, negative results and replication history so that each research cycle can inform the next.

A continuous research loop

Discovery → data acquisition → variable formation → hypothesis generation → testing → falsification → replication → improved questions.

The process is iterative rather than linear. A surprising result may send the system back to search for new sources, construct a different variable or design a better experiment.

Research principles

Open scope

No permanent restriction to one discipline, institution, dataset or predefined set of variables.

Evidence before conviction

Existing research is valuable evidence, not unquestionable authority. Founding assumptions must also be open to challenge.

Reproducibility

Record sources, methods, assumptions, uncertainty, failed attempts and the steps needed to independently verify a result.

Responsible access

Use lawful, accessible evidence. Respect privacy, licences, access controls and human autonomy. Research freedom does not authorise intrusion or harm.

Current implementation status

Early prototype — not yet the autonomous research system described above.

The currently running experiment is deliberately limited: it retrieves a small, predefined set of World Bank indicators for 2000–2023 and calculates exploratory cross-country correlations. It does not yet browse the open web autonomously, discover new data sources or variables, generate its own research agenda, or develop and validate new research methods.

This prototype is a starting point for testing automated data retrieval, reproducible analysis and scheduled execution. The intended next architectural steps are broader source discovery, a persistent knowledge layer, autonomous research planning, experimental tooling, independent validation and controlled self-improvement.
