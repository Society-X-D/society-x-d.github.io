SOCIETY-X is an evolving research initiative built around one ambition: explore the world without a fixed list of questions, discover overlooked variables, connect distant fields and test ideas that may not yet have been considered.

Not a search engine for familiar answers. A system designed to become an independent investigator — curious, sceptical, reproducible and willing to revise its own assumptions.

01 / The mission

Let the questions emerge from the evidence.

The research agenda should not be permanently defined by a person, discipline or dataset. SOCIETY-X should be able to notice what is missing, decide what is worth investigating and follow a promising question across conventional boundaries.

01 · DISCOVER

Explore without a fixed map

Search accessible public data, scientific literature, archives and other evidence. Detect gaps, anomalies and variables that existing frameworks may overlook.

02 · CONNECT

Cross the boundaries

Bring together observations from different fields, places and timescales. Look for relationships hidden by fragmented research.

03 · INVENT

Form original questions

Construct measurable variables and proxies, generate competing hypotheses and derive predictions that could prove them wrong.

04 · CHALLENGE

Attack its own conclusions

Search for counter-evidence, confounders, alternative explanations and methodological weaknesses before accepting a result.

05 · EXPERIMENT

Improve the method

Test new analytical strategies in isolated, reproducible experiments. Keep improvements only when they outperform a clear baseline.

06 · ACCUMULATE

Build durable knowledge

Preserve sources, code, uncertainty, failed attempts and replication history. Each cycle should make the next one more informed.

02 / The research loop

A system that keeps asking what comes next.

Research is not a one-way pipeline. Every result can expose a new gap, create a new variable or force a question to be reframed.

Discover→Collect evidence→Construct variables→Hypothesise→Test→Falsify→Replicate↺
03 / Guiding principles

Intellectual freedom. Methodological discipline.

A broad research scope is only useful if the system remains honest about evidence, limitations and the difference between an interesting pattern and a verified finding.

Open scope

No permanent restriction to a single discipline, source, question or predefined variable list.

Evidence before conviction

Established research matters, but no source or assumption is beyond scrutiny — including the system's own.

Reproducibility

Record provenance, methods, uncertainty, negative results and enough detail for independent checking.

Novelty must be earned

A pattern is not a discovery simply because it looks surprising. Compare prior work, test alternatives and seek independent replication.

Responsible access

Use lawful, accessible evidence. Respect privacy, licences, access controls and human autonomy.

Controlled self-improvement

Experiment freely in isolated branches; evaluate changes before adoption. Security, permissions and production governance remain protected.

05 / Where the project stands

The ambition is broad. The implementation is early.

SOCIETY-X is being built in stages. This portal distinguishes the long-term research vision from capabilities that have actually been implemented and tested.

Current implementation · Early prototype

Recurring cloud research cycles · every hour

The engine now runs automatically in GitHub Actions every hour, subject to available free Actions quota. Each cycle now samples OpenAlex's live multidisciplinary topic taxonomy, remembers surfaced topics, identifies cross-field links in scholarly works and searches Zenodo for candidate dataset records. These produce literature-derived research hypotheses and dataset leads. Quantitative analysis is still limited to implemented connectors; discovered records are not yet automatically validated or ingested.

Autonomous planning · early stage
STAGE 01 · FOUNDATION

Reliable research records

Reproducible execution, source provenance, transparent limitations and a public-facing research log.

STAGE 02 · DISCOVERY

Broader evidence access

Discover and catalogue independent public datasets and literature across multiple fields.

STAGE 03 · REASONING

Autonomous research planning

Generate questions, construct variables, compare explanations and prioritise experiments.

STAGE 04 · VALIDATION

Robust testing

Confounder analysis, robustness checks, out-of-sample tests and independent replication.

STAGE 05 · LEARNING

Measured self-improvement

Evaluate research strategies and code changes in isolated environments against explicit benchmarks.

STAGE 06 · CONTINUITY

A growing knowledge base

Retain evidence, methods and uncertainty so new research can build on what has already been tested.

The long-term direction

Do not decide in advance what the system will find.

Give it the tools to explore, the discipline to test, the memory to learn and the humility to admit when it does not know. The goal is not to manufacture impressive conclusions. It is to build a research process capable of finding out what is actually there.
