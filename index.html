How to interpret these results

The candidates below are exploratory and unverified. They show statistical patterns in a limited selection of indicators. They do not establish causality, novelty, or predictive power.

SOCIETY-X aims to report failed attempts, data limitations, and findings that contradict previous hypotheses. Stronger conclusions require independent data, robustness checks, and replication.

Data and methodology

Current data coverage: World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI) only. This first experiment uses a limited selection of WDI indicators for 2000–2023. It is a deliberately narrow starting point, not the intended boundary of SOCIETY-X research. Other independent sources have not yet been integrated.

For each year, the prototype calculates Pearson correlations across countries with available observations. These statistical associations are used to generate leads for further investigation; they are not explanations, causal findings, or a complete statistical review. The next stages should broaden the evidence base with independent datasets and test whether candidate patterns hold up.
